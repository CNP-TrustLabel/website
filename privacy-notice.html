CNP Data Protection Notice.

This notice explains the intended handling of contact data and uploaded product files in the CNP submission workflow.

Before accepting real submissions, replace the placeholder controller/contact details, confirm the legal basis, define the retention schedule and verify contracts and transfer safeguards with all service providers.

1. Controller and contact

Controller: [CNP legal entity / project owner].
Privacy contact: [privacy email].

2. Data processed

Contact details, brand and product information, selected validation level, uploaded product documents and any personal data contained within those files.

3. Purposes

To receive and assess submissions, communicate with the submitting brand, manage the validation pathway and maintain the relevant project record.

4. Legal basis

The applicable legal basis must be confirmed before launch and stated here. Depending on the relationship, processing may be necessary to take pre-contractual steps or perform a contract; other bases must be identified explicitly where used.

5. Data minimisation and confidentiality

Submitters should remove personal data unrelated to the product assessment. Confidential product information should be limited to what CNP needs for the selected review.

6. Recipients and service providers

Access should be limited to authorised CNP reviewers and approved service providers supporting storage, communication or specialist assessment. Any international transfers and safeguards must be described in the final notice.

7. Retention

Personal data and uploaded files should be retained only for the period necessary for the assessment, certification relationship, legal obligations and dispute management. The final retention periods must be documented before launch.

8. Rights

Individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, subject to the conditions of the GDPR.

9. Official references

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — GDPR
European Commission — GDPR principles

CNP is an independent scientific assessment framework in development. It does not replace legal cosmetic compliance, public-authority review or accredited certification.
