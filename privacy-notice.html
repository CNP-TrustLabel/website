1. Controller and contact

For pilot enquiries, contact the CNP project team at cnp.trust.label@gmail.com. The final legal identity of the controller must be published before commercial processing begins.

2. Data processed

Contact details, brand and product information, preferred validation level, email correspondence, attached product documents and any personal data contained within them.

3. Purpose

To receive and assess product submissions, communicate with the submitting organisation, define the validation pathway and maintain the relevant project record.

4. Data minimisation

Submitters should remove personal data that is unrelated to the product assessment and share only the confidential product information necessary for the requested review.

5. Access and confidentiality

Access should be limited to authorised CNP team members and approved specialists who need the information for the assessment. Commercial launch requires appropriate confidentiality and data-processing arrangements.

6. Retention

Files and correspondence should be retained only for the period necessary for the assessment, the certification relationship, legal obligations and dispute management. Final periods must be defined before launch.

7. Individual rights

Depending on the applicable legal basis and circumstances, individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

8. Contact

Questions about data protection or requests concerning submitted information can be sent to cnp.trust.label@gmail.com.
