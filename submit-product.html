FAQ

Before you send the email.

Do I need to send a physical product?
No. Bronze and Silver require digital documents only. For Gold, CNP requests samples later, after the protocol, quantity and shipping conditions are agreed.
Can the website attach my files automatically?
No. The email opens with the subject and message already prepared, but you must add attachments in your email application.
What if the files are too large?
Use a private, access-controlled cloud link and include the access instructions in the email. Do not use a public link for confidential documents.
How are personal data handled under the GDPR?
CNP uses submitted contact data and files for the stated assessment purpose. Share only necessary information, remove unrelated personal data and review the Data Protection Notice.
What should I do with highly confidential formula data?
Indicate the existence of confidential material and share it only through the agreed channel and under the appropriate confidentiality framework.

CNP is an independent scientific assessment framework in development. It does not replace legal cosmetic compliance, public-authority review or accredited certification.
