The CNP Standard

Open each area to see what CNP reviews.

The assessment checks relevance, traceability, consistency and proportionality—not simply whether a document exists.

01Formula & INCI+

The qualitative and quantitative formulation, final INCI and the role of relevant ingredients.

Typical inputsFormula, supplier specifications, ingredient concentrations and product format.
CNP checksConsistency between composition, intended use, safety information and claims.
OutputA structured formulation review and a list of clarification points.
02PIF, CPSR & core records+

The regulatory and technical documentation that frames the product.

Typical inputsPIF sections, CPSR, manufacturing and quality records, and relevant declarations.
CNP checksCompleteness, internal consistency, version control and alignment with the product submitted.
OutputA document-gap map and priority requests.
03Stability & microbiological quality+

Evidence that the finished product remains suitable throughout its intended life.

Typical inputsStability studies, packaging compatibility, challenge testing and microbiological specifications.
CNP checksWhether methods, time points, batches and acceptance criteria support the stated shelf life and use conditions.
OutputA quality-evidence assessment linked to the finished formulation.
04Claim substantiation+

The evidence used to support what the product communicates.

Typical inputsClaim list, literature, ingredient data, instrumental tests, consumer studies and expert assessments.
CNP checksClaim specificity, relevance of the tested material, method quality and strength of inference.
OutputAn approved-claim map showing supported, conditional and unsupported wording.
05In-silico validation+

Computational analyses used to add mechanistic or quantitative support.

Typical inputsExisting models, ingredient properties, formulation data and exposure or permeation inputs.
CNP checksModel applicability, assumptions, parameter traceability, sensitivity and limitations.
OutputA transparent model report with interpretation and boundaries of use.
06Experimental evidence+

New testing designed around the finished product and the claim.

Typical inputsAgreed protocol, product samples where required, controls and predefined outcomes.
CNP checksScientific relevance, reproducibility, controls, data quality and claim-level interpretation.
OutputA test report that links the result directly to the approved claim scope.

A standard, not a pay-to-claim system.

The selected level defines the depth of work, but the mark is released only when the evidence meets the relevant CNP criteria. A higher level does not automatically mean a better product; it means a deeper validation pathway.
