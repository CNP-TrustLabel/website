How CNP works

What happens, who acts and what the brand receives.

Each step has a defined purpose, responsibility and output.

01Submit

Submit the digital dossier.

The brand provides the product information needed to define the scope.

  • Formula and final INCI
  • Claims and existing substantiation
  • PIF/CPSR and relevant records
  • Stability, safety and efficacy data already available
Time1–2 working days
OutputIntake confirmation and product scope
02Assess

CNP performs the evidence assessment.

CNP reviews document quality, claim relevance and evidence gaps.

  • Consistency across formula, records and claims
  • Missing or outdated evidence
  • Questions requiring clarification
  • Recommended validation depth
Time5–10 working days
OutputAssessment memo and gap map
03Validate

The validation level is confirmed.

The brand and CNP agree Bronze, Silver or Gold based on the dossier and intended claims.

  • Bronze: documentation review
  • Silver: Bronze + in-silico validation
  • Gold: Bronze + experimental programme
  • In-silico work may also support Gold where useful
Time2–12 weeks
OutputValidation report and approved claim scope
04Release

CNP releases the final outcome.

Once criteria are met, CNP prepares the product-facing trust assets.

  • CNP level mark
  • Approved claims list
  • Certificate or assessment statement
  • Digital evidence page / QR-ready content
TimeAfter final approval
OutputVisible and traceable evidence package
How It Works

A clear division of responsibilities.

The brand provides

  • Accurate, current product information
  • Authorisation to share the submitted material
  • Timely responses to technical questions
  • Samples only when requested for Gold

CNP provides

  • Independent scientific review
  • A proportionate validation recommendation
  • Transparent assumptions and limitations
  • A clear final evidence outcome
