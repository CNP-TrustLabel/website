How CNP works

Four steps. One transparent pathway.

CNP starts from the digital dossier, assesses the evidence, confirms the appropriate validation depth and releases the final certification assets.

Submit Your ProductExplore the Four Steps

What changes between products?

Only Step 3. Bronze reviews existing evidence, Silver adds in-silico validation and Gold adds experimental testing.

Samples

Bronze and Silver: none. Gold: only after protocol approval.

The process

What happens, who acts and what the brand receives.

Each step has one clear purpose and one clear output.

01Submit

Submit the digital dossier.

The brand provides the controlled product information needed to define the assessment scope.

  • Formula and final INCI
  • PIF, CPSR and relevant records
  • Claims and existing support
  • Stability, safety and efficacy data already available
Time1–2 working days
OutputIntake confirmation and product scope
02Assess

CNP reviews the evidence package.

CNP checks consistency, maps each claim to evidence and identifies the remaining gaps.

  • Quality-standard review
  • Claim and evidence relevance
  • Version consistency
  • Recommended validation level
Time5–10 working days
OutputGap analysis and pathway recommendation
03Validate

Complete Bronze, Silver or Gold.

The selected level determines how far CNP continues beyond the shared documentation and claim review.

BronzeExisting evidence reviewSilverIn-silico validationGoldExperimental validation
Explore Validation Levels
TimeBronze 2–3 · Silver 4–6 · Gold 8–12 weeks
SamplesNone for Bronze/Silver; Gold only after protocol approval
04Release

Release the CNP outcome.

Once the selected requirements are met, CNP releases the traceable certification package.

  • Certificate and achieved level
  • Bronze, Silver or Gold mark
  • Approved claim list
  • QR-linked evidence summary
Time3–5 working days
OutputVisible, product-specific trust system
FAQ

Questions brands ask before starting.

Essential answers on samples, existing evidence, data protection and the relationship with cosmetic compliance.

Does CNP replace mandatory cosmetic compliance?
No. Legal compliance remains the responsibility of the company and the Responsible Person. CNP adds an independent scientific assessment and communication layer.
Do Bronze and Silver require a physical product?
No. Both pathways are completed from digital documents and data. Gold samples are requested only after the experimental protocol has been defined and agreed.
Can CNP use our existing studies?
Yes. CNP starts from the evidence already available and requests additional work only when it is needed to support the selected claims and level.
How are personal data and uploaded files handled under the GDPR?
The upload workflow is designed around purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation and confidentiality. Submitters should remove unnecessary personal data, confirm they are authorised to share the files and review the CNP Data Protection Notice before uploading. Access to the destination folder must be restricted to authorised reviewers.
Where should we begin?
Open the submission page, review the document checklist and upload the digital dossier. CNP will confirm the recommended level after the initial assessment.
