Documentation Review

CNP evaluates the dossier, claims and existing evidence.

  • Formula, INCI and technical records
  • Claim-to-evidence mapping
  • Quality and evidence-gap review
  • Approved claim wording and assessment outcome
Samples: No physical product sample is required.
Start with Bronze
4–6 weeks

In-Silico Validation

Bronze plus computational validation where suitable data and models exist.

  • Everything included in Bronze
  • Model selection and applicability review
  • Mechanistic or quantitative analysis
  • Sensitivity, limitations and model report
Samples: No physical product sample is required.
Start with Silver
8–12 weeks

Experimental Validation

Bronze plus an experimental programme managed by CNP or qualified partner laboratories.

  • Everything included in Bronze
  • Claim-specific experimental protocol
  • Testing of the finished product
  • Interpretation, report and approved claim scope
Samples: Samples are requested only after the protocol is approved.
Start with Gold
